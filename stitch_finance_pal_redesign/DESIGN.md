## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, high-confidence fintech aesthetic balancing electric dynamism with uncompromising financial clarity. Tailored for personal wealth tracking, smart budgeting, and visual savings goals, it targets ambitious individuals who seek empowerment and total ownership over their personal economy. 

The visual style merges modern precision with subtle glassmorphic depth. Deep midnight and slate surfaces anchor the dark canvas, while vivid electric blues and radiant cyan accents illuminate focal data points and primary interactive paths. Floating translucent panels, crisp contrast ratios, and approachable curved contours evoke security, precision, and forward momentum.

## Colors

The palette operates primarily in an immersive dark mode where deep navy obsidian (`#0b1120`) serves as the root canvas, avoiding flat dead blacks in favor of rich chromatic depth. 

Primary interactions, active tabs, and primary hero cards leverage `#0ea5e9` and `#0284c7`, creating electric intensity without introducing eye strain. Secondary and tertiary accents—vibrant sky (`#38bdf8`) and luminous cyan (`#06b6d4` / `#22d3ee`)—are reserved for progress fills, active indicators, and highlighted metrics.

Card surfaces layer upward in luminosity: foundational cards sit on `#111827`, elevated modules on `#1e293b`, and floating navigation bars utilize semi-transparent obsidian overlays (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`) combined with hairline glass borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). Financial statuses are explicitly partitioned: positive inflows use crisp mint (`#10b981`), while outflows and alerts utilize vivid rose (`#f43f5e`).

## Typography

The typographic architecture combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headings and numeric milestones with **Inter** for dense transactional tables, data labels, and systemic UI text. 

Numeric values and financial headlines demand maximum clarity and modern energy; Plus Jakarta Sans provides refined geometric weight distribution, keeping figures crisp and instantly readable. Inter ensures zero character ambiguity across dense sub-labels, input forms, timestamps, and secondary status rows.

Uppercase label roles (`label-caps`) use wider letter spacing (`0.08em`) to guarantee legibility on dark card headers and category tags. For primary currency displays, numeric strings should use tabular lining figures to maintain uniform visual alignment during rapid accounting scans.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 12-column grid on desktop screens, scaling to a 6-column grid on tablet and a single-column stacked container on mobile devices. 

Outer canvas margins scale from `1rem` (16px) on compact handheld views up to `3rem` (48px) on wide desktop views, anchoring the content centrally within a maximum container width of 1280px. Interior padding in primary dashboard hero cards remains uniform at `1.5rem` (24px) to retain breathing room around high-value financial statistics.

A strict 4px/8px modular base rhythm governs vertical cadences: micro-separations (such as icons paired with text) use `space-2xs` (4px) or `space-xs` (8px); adjacent transaction rows utilize `space-md` (16px) spacing; while distinct structural card modules are spaced by `space-lg` (24px) to preserve distinct visual chunks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal surfaces, luminous back-glows, and frosted glass elements rather than traditional muddy drop shadows.

1. **Surface 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0b1120`, forming the silent base environment.
2. **Surface 1 (Base Cards & Modules):** Solid `#111827` framed with a delicate, crisp outer border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
3. **Surface 2 (Elevated & Active Items):** Solid `#1e293b` paired with a soft directional shadow tinted by the canvas: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
4. **Surface Glass (Navigation, Filters & Floating Sheets):** Semi-opaque background (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`), an active `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and a subtle top-lit rim highlight (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
5. **Hero Gradient Elevation:** Main balance and net-worth cards feature an electric gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0284c7 0%, #0ea5e9 50%, #38bdf8 100%)`) accompanied by an ambient luminous glow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined `Rounded` geometry (level 2), striking an optimal balance between friendly, consumer-accessible curves and rigorous fintech reliability. 

Standard input components, compact chips, and transaction list items adopt `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) border radii. Large feature cards, modular goal containers, and hero banners utilize generous `1.5rem` (24px) corners, echoing modern mobile-first operating systems. 

Pill forms (full radius `9999px`) are intentionally reserved for badges, quick-action chips, folder navigation tabs, and primary action buttons, providing immediate tactile affordance across tap and click targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full electric blue gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0284c7, #0ea5e9)`) with white text, font weight 600, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 24px`. Subtle electric drop glow on hover or active press (`0 4px 14px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Glass surface with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, with cyan or bright white typography.
- **Icon Action Buttons:** Circular (`rounded-full`) or smooth rounded squares (`rounded-xl`), `40px x 40px`, backdrop blur with soft white hover rim.

### Badges & Chips
- Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). Category and tag chips use low-opacity tinted fills (e.g., cyan badge: `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)` background, `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`, text `#38bdf8`).
- Numerical pill counters sit flush right with subtle high-contrast fill.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- **Hero Balance Card:** Saturated electric blue base, white typography, inner sub-metric pills rendered in semi-transparent white overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) with rounded corners.
- **Transaction Cards:** Flat `#111827` surface with rounded corners (`16px`), containing circular emoji/merchant avatars on the left, primary metadata in vertical stacks, and high-visibility monetary figures on the right.
- **Goal / Savings Cards:** Bordered slate panels with integrated linear cyan progress meters (`height: 6px`, `rounded-full`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background set to `#111827`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Height of `48px`, font size 14px, interior horizontal padding `16px`.
- **Focus State:** Border color transitions to `#38bdf8` alongside a non-blur glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Floating Bottom Bar:** Fixed overlay anchored along the bottom viewport with safe-area spacing. Translucent slate background (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`), heavy backdrop blur (`20px`), rounded pills for active tabs, with top hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **List Dividers:** Hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with inset padding to match row content margins.

### Checkboxes & Switches
- Toggles utilize a smooth pill container (`width: 48px`, `height: 26px`) in deep slate `#1e293b`, moving to vibrant `#0ea5e9` when active. Thumb is pure white `#ffffff` with light dimensional elevation.
